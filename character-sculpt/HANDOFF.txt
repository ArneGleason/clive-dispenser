Take a turn at making a better Clive in https://github.com/ArneGleason/clive-dispenser. The goal is a mischievous gnome I’d want to print and put on a shelf. Focus this turn on his likeness, expression, and pose, and above all on the shape language and material character described in subjects/clive.md. The dispenser mechanics come later.

Start in character-sculpt/. If you are working locally, reuse an existing checkout of clive-dispenser in your active workspace or default project directory (fetching and pulling main if clean) before cloning a fresh copy. Read subjects/clive.md, WORKFLOW.md, LOG.md, FIELD-GUIDE.md, HYPOTHESES.md, the recent run notes, and any open sculpt discussion issues on GitHub (starting with Issue #3). Look through every saved Clive in the log and gallery and choose your own starting point (any earlier model, or a fresh start); say which you chose and why. Open the approved front, side, and back turnaround at character-sculpt/references/clive-turnaround.png (path from the repository root). If your checkout predates the image, fetch main or open the PNG directly at https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArneGleason/clive-dispenser/main/character-sculpt/references/clive-turnaround.png. Orbit your starting model, including high and low three-quarter views. Use your judgment to choose the change with the biggest visual payoff. A previous agent’s suggestion is a hypothesis you can test or challenge.

Make the change with the tools available to you. You can add or subtract volume, refine the cage, start from anatomical/garment reference meshes, use cloth simulation or sculpting, or try a different modeling method altogether. Render the parent and candidate with matched framing and light. Show the angle where your change helped most and the angle where it holds up least.

Contribute your turn to the repo. Save the editable candidate, screenshots, and a run record based on character-sculpt/RUN.md in a new experiments/ directory. Append a short entry to character-sculpt/LOG.md, add your screenshots to the gallery (including its GitHub browsing index), and refresh the root README’s showcase so visitors immediately understand the relay and want to take a turn. Sign your turn: put your model’s name, and the app you ran in, in the run record, the log’s turn roster, the gallery and the README table. Preserve earlier runs and credit any findings you build on. If you have write access, commit the contribution or open a PR; otherwise give me a patch or downloadable handoff.

Leave three things for the next agent: what you observed and from which view; what you discovered by testing, including failed ideas or regressions; and the next improvement you’d try, with a way to tell whether it worked. If your test taught something reusable, add or revise a finding in character-sculpt/FIELD-GUIDE.md with what worked, where it failed, and a link to the evidence. Bring open ideas or conflicting results to the GitHub discussion if relevant. I’ll give my verdict on the result; the next contributor is free to start from any Clive.
