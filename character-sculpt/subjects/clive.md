# Clive · subject brief

**Goal.** Make a shelf figurine that reads as this particular mischievous gnome, even in unpainted plastic. First get an exterior Arne likes. A later project phase will make a head press dispense one Tic Tac behind him.

**Visual target.** The owner-supplied image titled *CLIVE — SCULPTURE TURNAROUND*, also saved as *Whimsical Winking Ceramic Gnome Turnaround.png*, shows the approved front, side, and back on one sheet. [Open the approved original](../references/clive-turnaround.png). The experiment PNGs and JPEGs in this repo are renders of previous models. Treating them as the original would progressively train later attempts on earlier mistakes.

| Read in the sheet | What to check while orbiting |
|---|---|
| One eye open, the other squeezed shut above a lifted cheek and a crooked, toothy grin | Follow that cheek as it turns into the lower eyelid and lifts the moustache. Test from below as well as above. |
| Nose ahead of the toes, nearly horizontal upper back, raised seat | Follow the weight from the head through the shoulders and hips. Check the outer silhouette and the volume inside it. |
| Both hands gripping the lowered waistband, trouser folds gathered above the boots | Look through the hand–fabric contact from a low oblique view. |
| Thick hooked red cap, ivory flowing beard and moustache, blue smock, brown boots, pale plinth | Read the large masses at thumbnail size, then inspect the folds. Preserve the bare rear without a sign. |

The overhead shape, underside, unseen side of the face, and internal construction are inferred. Record consequential guesses. Preserve the eye assignment shown in the front view.
